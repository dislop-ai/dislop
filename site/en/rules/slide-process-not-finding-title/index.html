Process-label slide title instead of takeaway

slide-process-not-finding-title Executive Slide Deck & Board Readout (executive-deck) warning markdown, pptx Markdown card
HOW TO JUDGE

Replace 'Analysis of X' with the one-sentence answer your audience should remember if they read only the headline.

What this rule catches

Topic-label headline names the work done ('Analysis of X') instead of stating the finding.

Before and after examples

Original (flagged)
## Analysis of Q3 Churn Drop (6.2% to 4.1%)
Fallback trap (still sounds like AI)
## Reimagining Customer Retention Excellence (6.2% to 4.1%)
Clean version
## Q3 Customer Churn Dropped from 6.2% to 4.1%

Edit note: Put the 34% -> 19% finding directly in the heading instead of wrapping it in 'Analysis of...'.

Run locally via CLI

npx --yes dislop explain slide-process-not-finding-title
npx --yes dislop check . --pack executive-deck
