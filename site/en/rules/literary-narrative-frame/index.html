Sweeping era opener in personal essay

literary-narrative-frame Literary Essay & Longform Narrative (literary-essay) warning markdown, text, html, docx Markdown card
HOW TO JUDGE

Delete the opening era backdrop and the closing takeaway; start directly inside the concrete time, place, or object.

What this rule catches

Sweeping era opener ('In an era defined by...') or uplifting takeaway closer wraps a personal essay in an LLM template.

Before and after examples

Original (flagged)
In an era defined by relentless notifications, the street stays quiet before seven except for the milk truck. Moving forward, protecting small pockets of stillness remains essential for living with intention.
Fallback trap (still sounds like AI)
Notifications beep. Milk truck passes. Stillness stays.
Clean version
Before seven the street stays quiet except for the milk truck.

Edit note: Cut the sweeping era opener and closing moral while keeping the source scene verbatim.

Run locally via CLI

npx --yes dislop explain literary-narrative-frame
npx --yes dislop check . --pack literary-essay
