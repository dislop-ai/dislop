dislop / Rules / low-burstiness-vapor

Flat sentence rhythm with zero concrete facts

low-burstiness-vapor Business Brief & Technical Proposal (business-brief) info markdown, text Markdown card
HOW TO JUDGE

Cut abstract summary sentences in half, vary sentence length naturally, and anchor every kept claim with a number, file, date, or concrete failure mode.

What this rule catches

Multi-sentence passage combines robotically uniform sentence lengths (low burstiness CV < 0.24) with near-zero concrete anchors (numbers, dates, files, or verifiable actions).

Before and after examples

Original (flagged)
The platform focuses on improving overall developer productivity. System modules maintain clean architectural boundaries. Team workflows follow consistent operational standards. Product releases progress steadily toward core goals. Customer feedback reflects positive adoption trends.
Fallback trap (still sounds like AI)
Productivity rises. Boundaries hold. Releases ship.
Clean version
Cutting the home bundle from 420 KB to 145 KB dropped mobile first paint to 0.9s, and 3 backend engineers now rotate weekly via `ops/oncall.yaml`.

Edit note: Dual-gated on both low burstiness AND low concreteness; never fabricate missing metrics during editing.

npx --yes dislop explain low-burstiness-vapor
npx --yes dislop check . --pack business-brief
