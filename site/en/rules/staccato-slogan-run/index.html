Staccato slogan chain

staccato-slogan-run Business Brief & Technical Proposal (business-brief) warning markdown, text, html, docx, pptx Markdown card
HOW TO JUDGE

Combine the fragments into one factual sentence naming the cause and effect, or cut purely decorative rhythm beats.

What this rule catches

Three or more consecutive ultra-short slogan fragments mimic dramatic copywriting cadence.

Before and after examples

Original (flagged)
Signals drop. Photos vanish. Audits stall. Offline sync keeps all 120 inspection photos queued locally.
Fallback trap (still sounds like AI)
Offline sync isn't a fallback — it's field resilience for 120 photos.
Clean version
When cellular coverage drops in basement vaults, offline sync queues up to 120 inspection photos locally until reconnection.

Edit note: Turn dramatic fragments into the actual operating condition while keeping '120 inspection photos'.

Run locally via CLI

npx --yes dislop explain staccato-slogan-run
npx --yes dislop check . --pack business-brief
