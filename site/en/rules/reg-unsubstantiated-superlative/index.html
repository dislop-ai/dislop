Unsubstantiated '100% / zero-risk' claim

reg-unsubstantiated-superlative External & Regulated Communications (regulated-comms) warning markdown, text, html, docx, pptx Markdown card
HOW TO JUDGE

Replace absolute promises with verifiable scope, encryption/audit standards, or contracted SLA numbers.

What this rule catches

Absolute or superlative claim creates legal/compliance exposure where scoped SLA terms belong.

Before and after examples

Original (flagged)
Our AES-256 vault is 100% secure with a zero-risk migration (99.95% monthly SLA).
Fallback trap (still sounds like AI)
Our AES-256 vault is an unbreakable fortress for enterprise data (99.95% monthly SLA).
Clean version
Backups use AES-256 encryption at rest and carry a 99.95% monthly uptime SLA.

Edit note: State the actual architecture and contracted 99.95% SLA instead of '100% safe' promises.
