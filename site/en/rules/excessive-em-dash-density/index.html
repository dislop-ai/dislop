Too many em-dashes

excessive-em-dash-density Literary Essay & Longform Narrative (literary-essay) info markdown, text, html, docx Markdown card
HOW TO JUDGE

Convert most parenthetical dashes into commas or periods; save a single dash for a genuine turn.

What this rule catches

High em-dash (— / ——) frequency creates breathless LLM parenthetical rhythm.

Before and after examples

Original (flagged)
The station clock — brass-rimmed — stopped at 4:10 — right before the snow — while three passengers — coats buttoned — waited outside.
Fallback trap (still sounds like AI)
Brass clock. 4:10 sharp. Snow falling. Three passengers waiting.
Clean version
The brass-rimmed station clock stopped at 4:10, right before the snow, while three passengers waited outside in buttoned coats.

Edit note: Chinese double half-em —— counts as one dash; only sustained saturation (> 4 per 300 words) triggers.
