eng-vague-action-item Engineering RFC, PR & Postmortem (engineering-spec) info markdown, text, docx Markdown card
HOW TO JUDGE

Rewrite as a binary-verifiable ticket: which service, what threshold or test case, who owns it, and by what date.

What this rule catches

Wishful action item lacks a concrete target component, threshold, owner, or due date.
