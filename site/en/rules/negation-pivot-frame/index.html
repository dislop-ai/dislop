"Not X, but Y" slogan frame

negation-pivot-frame Business Brief & Technical Proposal (business-brief) warning markdown, text, html, docx, pptx Markdown card
HOW TO JUDGE

Delete the negated first half ('Not X,'). If the remaining clause ('Y') holds no measurement, mechanism, or real correction, rewrite or cut the whole sentence.

What this rule catches

Second-order 'Not X, but Y' rhetorical pivot elevates an abstract slogan instead of stating a concrete fact.
