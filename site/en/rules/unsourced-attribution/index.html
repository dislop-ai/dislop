Unsourced claim

unsourced-attribution Business Brief & Technical Proposal (business-brief) warning markdown, text, html, docx, pptx Markdown card
HOW TO JUDGE

Name the exact dataset, report year, or footnote [n]; if no source exists, delete the claim.

What this rule catches

Vague authority claim (studies show) without a named source, link, or citation.

Before and after examples

Original (flagged)
Industry research shows that 70% of enterprise decks fail basic accessibility checks.
Fallback trap (still sounds like AI)
Everyone knows most enterprise decks fail basic accessibility checks.
Clean version
In our audit of 48 internal Q3 review decks, 31 contained at least one low-contrast caption (< 4.5:1).

Edit note: Cite an actual sample size and defect definition, or link the external study.

Run locally via CLI

npx --yes dislop explain unsourced-attribution
npx --yes dislop check . --pack business-brief
