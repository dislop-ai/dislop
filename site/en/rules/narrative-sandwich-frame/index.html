Macro-trend opener or future-gazing closer

narrative-sandwich-frame Business Brief & Technical Proposal (business-brief) warning markdown, text, html, docx Markdown card
HOW TO JUDGE

Cut the opening trend preamble and closing cheerleading; start on the concrete fact or decision and end on the next action or limit.

What this rule catches

Macro-trend opener, future-facing closer, or dense transition-marker cluster ('Furthermore/Moreover/Additionally') follows the stock LLM essay sandwich.

Before and after examples

Original (flagged)
As organizations navigate rapid digital transformation, CI pipelines face new demands. Furthermore, static checks are growing. Moreover, docs matter. Additionally, workflows evolve. Looking ahead, teams that adapt will thrive.
Fallback trap (still sounds like AI)
Pipelines change. Checks grow. Teams adapt.
Clean version
Adding static doc checks to CI in November caught configuration typos 18 minutes earlier per PR; 4 more repos join next week.

Edit note: Cut the macro preamble, stacked transitions, and future-facing closer while keeping all source facts intact.
