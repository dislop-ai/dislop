Self-answered rhetorical Q&A

rhetorical-qa-cadence Business Brief & Technical Proposal (business-brief) warning markdown, text, html, docx, pptx Markdown card
HOW TO JUDGE

Delete the staged question and state the cause-and-effect directly in one sentence.

What this rule catches

Formulaic self-answered rhetorical question ('Why...? Because...' / 'The result?') mimics LLM blog hooks.

Before and after examples

Original (flagged)
Why do internal dashboards collect dust after launch? Because they show vanity totals instead of next actions. The result? Teams export CSVs every Monday.
Fallback trap (still sounds like AI)
Dashboards fail. Numbers pile up. Teams export CSVs.
Clean version
Internal dashboards go unused when they show vanity totals without next actions, so teams end up exporting CSVs every Monday.

Edit note: FAQ headings (## Why...? followed by an answer paragraph) and reported questions stay clean.

Run locally via CLI

npx --yes dislop explain rhetorical-qa-cadence
npx --yes dislop check . --pack business-brief
